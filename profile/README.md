# Bymii

a better you & me, with ai

Bymii is an AI agent on your Mac. You say what you need; it works in the background and hands back the finished file, page, or email.

## Bubbles

When a task finishes the way you want, keep it as a Bubble. Next time, drop it in with a sentence and Bymii does it the same way - for example, 12 receipts -> `Expense report - November.pdf`, followed next time by `"December, same as last time"`.

Bubbles are plain readable files on your Mac. Open them, edit them, back them up, or share them with a friend.

## Local and flexible

- Everything on your Mac works without an account and keeps working if the cloud is down.
- Use an AI plan or API key you already pay for, or use Bymii AI.
